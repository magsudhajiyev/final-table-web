If you play online, you already know your numbers. VPIP, PFR, 3-bet
percentage, how often you fold to a c-bet — your tracker computes all of it
while you play, and a HUD paints it right on the table. You know exactly what
kind of player you are, and exactly what kind of players you're up against.

If you play live, you know none of that.

That's not a small gap. It's the single biggest reason live players plateau:
you can't fix a leak you can't measure. You *feel* like you might be folding
too much from the big blind, or 3-betting too light from early position — but
feelings aren't data, and a night's worth of hands blurs together by the time
you're in the parking lot.

The reason the gap exists is simple: the software that computes these stats
was built for the desktop. It reads hand histories that online sites hand you
as text files. There is no hand history at a live table — just you, the felt,
and your memory. And nobody's running a laptop with PokerTracker open next to
their chips.

## Why "just remember it" doesn't work

People try to self-track. They keep a mental note ("I've been card dead"), or
scribble the occasional big hand in their phone's notes app. It never
produces real stats, for three reasons:

- **Stats need denominators, not anecdotes.** [VPIP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_in_poker#VPIP)
  isn't "hands I remember playing" — it's *voluntarily-played hands ÷ hands
  you were dealt*. You can't reconstruct the denominator from memory.
- **Position changes everything.** A 22% VPIP is tight under the gun and
  nitty on the button. A single blended number hides the leak. Real tracking
  is per-position.
- **The interesting stats are opportunity-based.** Your fold-to-3-bet only
  counts hands where you *faced* a 3-bet. Your steal percentage only counts
  unopened pots from the cutoff, button, or small blind. You cannot eyeball
  these.

## The number most apps get wrong

A quick tell for whether a "poker stats app" is real: ask how it calculates
VPIP. If it divides by *hands played*, it's broken — everyone comes out around
100%, because "hands played" already means "hands you played." The correct
denominator is **hands dealt minus walks** (a [walk](https://www.pokernews.com/poker-terms/walk.htm)
is when everyone folds to the big blind, who never got a chance to act).
Final Table uses the correct denominator, per position, which is why the
numbers actually reflect your game.
